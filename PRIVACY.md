# Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the mftlrs app, an open source file manager for Android based on Material Files.

## Information collection

mftlrs does not collect, store or send any personal information to its developers or to third parties. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash reporting services.

## Data on your device

- Files you open, copy, move or edit stay on your device or on the servers you connect to.
- Settings, including saved servers and their passwords, are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
- The app connects to the network only when you use network features: FTP, SFTP, SMB or WebDAV servers that you add, or the built-in FTP server that you start.

## Permissions

The app asks for access to files, notifications and network because a file manager needs them. This access is used only for the functions you start in the app.

## Crash reports

Crash reports may be added in a future version. They will be sent only after you confirm it in a dialog, and this policy will be updated before that.

## Changes to this policy

Changes to this policy are published in this file in the app's repository.

## Contact

Please open an issue in the app's GitHub repository.
